## 🗺️ Roadmap

Here is a look at our current development roadmap as we transition from a solo experience to a full-fledged online battleground.

### 🟡 Short-Term Goals (The Multiplayer Foundation)
- 🔄 **Basic Multiplayer Setup:** Integrate Photon PUN 2 for robust master server connections and room-based matchmaking.
- 👥 **Player Synchronization:** Accurately sync player positions, rotations, and movement across the network.
- 🔫 **Networked Combat:** Replicate weapon firing, projectiles, and damage systems so players can battle each other.
- 🤖 **Enemy AI Sync:** Ensure AI enemies are managed by the Master Client and act consistently across all connected screens.

### 🟢 Long-Term Goals (Expanding the Universe)
- 👤 **3rd Person Perspective (TPS):** Transition to support 3rd person character models, complete with full-body animations and inverse kinematics.
- 🌍 **Different Maps:** Design and implement a variety of new, distinct environments and arenas for players to fight in.
- 🏆 **Game Modes:** Introduce team-based combat modes (e.g., Team Deathmatch) and Co-op survival against waves of AI.
- 🎒 **Custom Loadouts:** Allow players to select their weapons and tactical gear before deploying into the map.
